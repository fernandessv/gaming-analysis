# 📊 Retention Analysis — Behavioral Data & Competitive Research

**Objective:**  
To analyze user retention behavior based on internal data and explore external retention strategies (rescue bonuses) used by competing online casinos in Malaysia. The goal is to identify actionable insights to improve loyalty and mitigate churn risk.

---

## 📈 Part 1: Behavioral Data Analysis

**Context:**  
Excel-based analysis of user engagement and retention patterns, focusing on metrics like cohort behavior, frequency of activity, and revenue contribution.

**Key Analyses:**  
- Retention by cohort and play frequency  
- Churn identification  
- Average revenue per user (ARPU)  
- Segmentation of at-risk and loyal players

**Tools Used:**  
- Excel (filters, pivot tables, KPI tracking)  
- Optional extensions: Power BI or Python (not included here)

---

## 🧠 Part 2: Market Research — Rescue Bonus Offers

**Context:**  
A competitive benchmarking study of 5 leading Malaysian online casinos, highlighting their use of “rescue bonuses” as player retention tools.

**Insights Covered:**  
- Frequency, caps, and wagering requirements for each bonus  
- Differentiated strategies targeting casual vs. high-rolling players  
- Strategic implications of bonus structure on user behavior

**Key Takeaways:**  
- Daily bonuses (e.g., 12Play) appeal to casual players  
- Weekly high-cap bonuses (e.g., BK8, Maxim88) target high-value users  
- Wagering requirements vary significantly and impact bonus attractiveness

---

## 🛠 Recommendations:

- Combine behavioral segmentation with bonus targeting (customized rescue offers)
- Consider tiered bonus systems aligned to user value and frequency
- Leverage competitor structures to position your offer competitively in the market
